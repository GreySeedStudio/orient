Who we are

Great products begin long before production.

For more than 50 years, we have partnered with some of the world's leading fashion brands, combining creativity, technical expertise and manufacturing excellence to bring collections to life.

From design development and sourcing to sampling, production and delivery, we provide an end-to-end partnership built on quality, transparency and long-term trust — across womenswear, childrenswear and men's shirting.

What we do

From concept to collection.

A fully integrated manufacturing ecosystem supporting every stage of the product journey — design, fabric, embellishment, print, production and quality — almost entirely in-house.

All capabilities →
01

Design & Product Development

We work as an extension of our partners' creative studios — trend research, design, fit and technical development.

02

Fabric Innovation & Sourcing

Over 60 mill partners across India, Vietnam, China and Italy, developing fabrics to each brand's aesthetic and performance needs.

03

Advanced Manufacturing

Five facilities across Delhi NCR with automated pintuck, cuff and collar systems engineered for consistency at scale.

04

Embroidery & Surface Ornamentation

CAD embroideries, chemical lace, cutwork and sequin work across 1,200+ embroidery heads — computerised in-house since 1998.

05

Digital Printing

A state-of-the-art in-house facility since 2021 — high-definition pigment and reactive printing with reduced water consumption.

06

AI-Assisted Development

AI across line optimisation, fit accuracy and automated SAM estimation — with tailor testing and grading five times faster.
