Our capabilities

From concept to collection.

Fifty years of bringing one more step of the process in-house — embroidery, lace, print — until very little needs to leave the building. Our design teams work alongside global brands to interpret trends, develop fabrics and engineer garments before production begins.

What we make

Womenswear, childrenswear and men's shirting.

Whether producing elevated everyday essentials or intricately detailed fashion collections, our approach combines craftsmanship with innovation to create garments that exceed expectations — while working towards a sustainable fashion future.

End to end

Ten capabilities, almost all in-house.

Transforming ideas into commercially successful collections through trend research, design expertise and technical development. We work as an extension of our partners' creative studios, ensuring consistency, comfort and craftsmanship in every garment — a design-driven approach led by heads with 20 and 38 years at the company.

Developing and sourcing premium fabrics tailored to each brand's aesthetic, performance and commercial requirements. Over 60 mill partners across India, Vietnam, China and Italy. All cotton is sourced as Better Cotton, organic or US cotton; the majority of our polyester is recycled; and viscose and modal are traceable through the TextileGenesis platform.

Five facilities across Delhi NCR, established between 1991 and 2017, equipped with automated technologies for precision, efficiency and consistent quality — including automated pintuck machines, automatic cuff machines and automatic collar-making machines. Combined capacity of 10 million units a year. More than 3,000 sewing machines run across our facilities.

Computerised in-house since 1998. Specialists in CAD embroideries, chemical lace, cutwork, sequin embroideries and intricate embellishment — over 1,200 embroidery heads with fully automated beading, sequencing and piping capabilities under one roof, a level of in-house capability that is rare anywhere in NCR.

A state-of-the-art in-house facility established in 2021. High-definition pigment and reactive printing delivers vibrant colour, reduced water consumption and faster turnaround, with greater creative flexibility in print development.

Our crochet lace facility, established in 1995, allows us to create unique lace developments using a wide range of thread counts, yarns and constructions — giving partners exclusive trims that cannot be sourced off the shelf.

Our commitment to quality is backed by meticulous in-line checks, thorough final inspections and an in-house testing lab approved by nearly all our global customers. Third-party testing is used only where specifically required, and AQL levels are set to each customer’s requirements.

Manufacturing responsibly through globally recognised standards and measurable initiatives. Every factory is SLCP verified with an Outstanding rating, recognised by leading US brands, and carries Higg FEM verification averaging above 89%. See all certifications →

Reliable sourcing, production and logistics supporting brands across more than ten export markets in North America, Europe, the UK, Asia and Australia. Backed by AEO Tier III certification for secure international trade and customs compliance.

AI runs across production line optimisation, lean manufacturing, design iterations, fit accuracy, automated SAM estimation and workforce skill assessment — using 3D design modules including CLO and Browzwear alongside generative AI. In practice that means training and recruitment assessment five times faster, intelligent planning of production lines for seamless turnovers, and advanced report analysis.
