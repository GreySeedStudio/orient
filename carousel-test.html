Prototype

Certifications as looping carousels.

Same content and card styling as the live Sustainability page — only the layout changes. Use the toggle at the top to flip between this and the current grid.

Each carousel loops endlessly in both directions. Arrows move one card at a time; you can also drag, swipe, or scroll sideways with a trackpad. Hold Shift and scroll on a mouse.

Worth deciding

A few things to look at while you try it.

Groups 2 and 3 only have four cards

On a wide screen roughly three and a half fit on view, so there is barely anything to scroll. The carousel earns its place in group 1 with seven cards; in the other two it may just add a control that does very little. Merging all fifteen into one carousel is an option.

Hiding content behind a control

The grid shows every certification at once, which is what a buyer doing due diligence usually wants — they scan for the one standard they care about. A carousel means scrolling to find it. It looks better; it is slightly harder to audit.

The loop has no start or end

Because it repeats endlessly, there is no natural signal that you have seen everything. The thin progress bar under each carousel is there to help, but a counter such as "3 / 7" may be clearer if you prefer.

Print and search

Cards off-screen are still in the page, so browser find and search engines see them all. Printing will clip them, which matters if buyers print this page for audit files. Easy to fix with a print stylesheet if we go ahead.
